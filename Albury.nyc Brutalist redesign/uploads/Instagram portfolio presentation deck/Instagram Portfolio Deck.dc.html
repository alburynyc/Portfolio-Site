01
Discover Conversion
Migrating 5 million+ debit customers to a new payment network, without breaking the payments they depend on.
Context & Role
Design Lead · Concept → Launch

Capital One's acquisition of Discover meant every checking customer was issued a new debit card and moved onto the Discover payment network. There was no opt-out, and this business decision would have required significant work for the customer in the current state of the experience.

5M+
Checking customers reissued a new debit card
The Problem
A new card number breaks every connected payment.

Recurring subscriptions and connected apps tied to the old card would silently fail. Customers had no built-in way to find what was connected, let alone move it.

The Solution
A guided switching experience.

I designed a flow that walked customers through moving every connected payment and subscription from their old card to the new one — no digging through statements required.

How It Works
Awareness
Activation
Guided Payment Switcher
Results
$50B+
in purchase volume retained — exceeding our 70% retention goal in under a year
"Customers named the switching experience the high point of the entire card transition" — moderated post-launch interviews
Impact Built to Last

Though scoped as a one-time reissue, I designed the guided switching flow to outlive the launch. Today, every customer with a lost or stolen debit card is routed through the same experience to reconnect their payments.

Additionally key components developed for this experience have been contributed back to the Capital One Design System.

02
Capital One Cash
A concept for personal, no-app-required gifting between customers and anyone in their contacts.
The Insight
Design Lead + 1 · Concept

A long-term study with our design research partners surfaced two person-to-person moments Capital One wasn't serving well: allowances and gifts. With another team already building a teen checking account, I focused my exploration on gifting.

Allowances owned elsewhere
Gifts my focus
The Opportunity
Digital gifting today is transactional, not personal.
Pain Point 01
Apps like Venmo require both sender and recipient to have an account.
Pain Point 02
The moment itself has no personality — it's a transfer, not a gift.
The Solution
Capital One Cash

Customers create a fully customizable digital gift card and send it to anyone in their contacts — regardless of whether they bank with us. Recipients authenticate with just a phone number, then load the cash to a digital wallet and spend it anywhere.

How It Works
01
Personalize the card
02
Send within a conversation
03
Claim with a phone number
Why It's Different
Recipients never need a Capital One account to receive or spend the gift.
Full customization keeps the personality that a plain transfer strips away.
The conversation happens wherever customers already talk — unmoderated by the bank.
Vision Forward

As a concept, Capital One Cash stakes a claim in a P2P moment banks have largely ceded to peer apps. The same personalized, no-account-required model could extend naturally toward allowances — the other underserved moment our research surfaced.
