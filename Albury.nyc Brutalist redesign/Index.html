About

Garrett Albury is a Senior Product Designer based in New York. He currently designs financial products at Capital One at scale, focused on debit card experiences used by millions of customers.

Outside of work, he writes and edits coolstuff.nyc, an independent, design-forward weekly newsletter, coolstuff.nyc.
